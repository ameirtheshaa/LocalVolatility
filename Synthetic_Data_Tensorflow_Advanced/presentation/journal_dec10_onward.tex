\begin{frame}{23/07/2026 --- the $K{=}0$ fix: \texttt{exp\_k} ansatz}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.54\linewidth}
      \gold{The ansatz change}
      \begin{itemize}\small
        \item Old: $\tilde\varphi = 1-e^{-N_c}$, $N_c=\mathrm{softplus}\ge0$ ---
              open at 1, so $C_{\mathrm{NN}}(0,T)<S_0$ structurally
        \item New: $\tilde\varphi = \exp(-k\,M)$,\;
              $M=\mathrm{softplus}(a+kH)$,\;
              $a=\log(e^{K_{\max}/S_0}-1)$,\; $H$ linear
        \item $\tilde\varphi(0)=e^{0}=1 \;\Rightarrow\; C_{\mathrm{NN}}(0,T)=S_0$ \red{exactly}
        \item $M(\cdot,0)=K_{\max}/S_0 \;\Rightarrow\;$ correct deep-ITM slope $+$ unit mass
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.42\linewidth}
      \gold{Why the old form couldn't}
      \begin{itemize}\small
        \item $\tilde\varphi(0)=1 \Leftrightarrow N_c(0)\to+\infty$: softplus never reaches it
        \item Existing soft penalty $\lambda_{k0}$: gradient $\propto e^{-2N_c}\to0$ as $\tilde\varphi\to1$ --- cannot close the gap
        \item \texttt{exp\_k} puts the exact boundary at $K{=}0$ \emph{by construction} (no penalty, no extra loss)
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

              \gold{worse} ($7.9\to10.4$). The truth sits \emph{between} the two
              anchorings: the sticky-strike\,/\,sticky-delta spectrum
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.48\linewidth}
      \gold{Why.} After the spot falls, paths sit in the \emph{low-strike} part of a
      surface pinned at calibration $\Rightarrow$ they read the higher low-strike
      volatility $\Rightarrow$ too much dispersion $\Rightarrow$ over-priced.
      \begin{itemize}\scriptsize
        \item The \gold{Hagan et al.\ (2002)} critique --- LV moves the smile
              \emph{opposite} to spot --- in our own numbers
        \item Corroborated by the term structure: the excess \emph{decays with maturity}
              (next slide), as long-dated paths average the anchoring error away
        \item \red{For the paper}: part of the residual reprice gap is a
              local-volatility \emph{model-class} property, not calibration quality ---
              \emph{better training cannot remove it}
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}
